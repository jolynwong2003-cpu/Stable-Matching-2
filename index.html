How Gale-Shapley works

In each round, every unmatched donor proposes to their most preferred recipient they haven't yet tried. A recipient who receives a proposal will tentatively hold it — but only if it's better than their current hold. If a better offer arrives, the previously held donor is released back to the free pool.

This continues until every donor is matched. The result is donor-optimal stable: every donor gets the best possible partner they could have in any stable matching, and no pair has a mutual incentive to defect from their assignment.

In real kidney exchange programs, preferences encode compatibility scores based on blood type, HLA tissue matching, waiting time, and medical urgency.
